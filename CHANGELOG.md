## [1.0.0] - 2026-04-07

### Added
- Screenshot capture from menu bar
- Styled screenshot copy to clipboard
- Open images from clipboard or file
- Launch at login support
- Keyboard shortcuts (Cmd+Ctrl+2, Shift+Cmd+2)
- PNG export functionality
